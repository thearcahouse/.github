> It helps collectors research what to buy, record what they own, and understand what it's worth over time.

<br>

## The Idea

> **A product tells you *what something is.***
> A reference number. A model name. A vintage. The same for every owner, everywhere.

> **An asset tells you *what you actually own.***
> Its condition. Its provenance. What you paid, where, and when. How the market has moved since.

Most tools stop at the product. **ARCA is built around the asset** — the specific piece in your possession, with its own history and its own value.

The relationship with an asset doesn't end at checkout. Neither does ARCA.

**After you buy** &nbsp; `Record` → `Track` → `Understand`

</div>

<br>

| Capability | What it means for you |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Research** | Context on references, models and makers before you commit |
| **Historical pricing** | See how comparable pieces have traded over time |

| **Valuation** | An informed view of what your collection may be worth today |
| **Portfolio view** | Every category in one collection, not six spreadsheets |
| **Intelligence** | Market context and AI-assisted insight tied to the pieces you own |

## Anatomy of an Asset

What a single ARCA record holds. *Illustrative example:*

| Field | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Category** | Watch |
| **Identity** | Steel chronograph · Ref. 0000 · c. 2019 |
| **Condition** | Excellent, light wear |
| **Completeness** | Box, papers, spare links |
| **Provenance** | Authorised dealer → first owner |
| **Acquisition** | Private sale, 2023 |
| **Service** | Manufacturer service, 2024 |
| **Documents** | Warranty card, invoice, service receipt |
| **Market** | Comparable sales, price history, trend |
| **Estimated value** | A range, with the basis shown |

A product listing stops at **Identity**. Everything below it is what makes the piece *yours*.

## Trust & Privacy

A record of valuable possessions is sensitive information. ARCA is being designed with that in mind:

- **Private by default.** Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.
- **You own your data.** Export your records whenever you want.
- **Values come with their basis.** Estimates show the context behind them, not just a number.
